Before we begin

  • What the camera measures. With your permission, Calm Breath uses your camera to estimate your breathing rate and heart-rate variability (the same kind of signal a pulse oximeter picks up, measured optically from tiny face movements and skin-tone changes). The SmartSpectra SDK processes video frames locally in this app.
  • Nothing is stored. No video, images, or measurements are saved to disk, ever. Closing the window discards everything from the session. There are no accounts and no analytics in this app.
  • SDK telemetry. The SmartSpectra SDK can send a small aggregate diagnostic summary (session duration, frame rate, SDK version — no video, no metric values, no identifiers) to Presage's servers. Calm Breath keeps this off by default; you can opt in below. See the SDK Telemetry & Privacy doc.
  • Not a medical device. Calm Breath is a wellness and relaxation demo. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition.

The camera turns on only after you press Start, and only while a session runs. An indicator stays visible; Stop ends it immediately.
